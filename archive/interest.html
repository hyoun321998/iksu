Expression of Interest

Are You In?

Connect your Kick account to verify you're a real creator and add your name to the list of people who want to see an official Kick Streamers Union formed.

⚠ Setup required: Open config.js and fill in your Supabase credentials and Kick OAuth Client ID. See the comments in that file for step-by-step instructions.

What This Is

This is an expression of interest — not a binding commitment. We are trying to establish how much support exists among Kick creators for forming an official union.

If we gather enough interest from the creator community, we will move forward with formally establishing the IKSU, electing creator representatives, and opening negotiations with Kick.

Your Kick account is authenticated directly through Kick's own login system — the same way you log in to Kick itself. Your username, profile picture, and a link to your channel will be shown publicly on the Who's Interested page.

What Happens Next

  • You log in via Kick — your identity is verified by Kick directly
  • Your profile appears publicly on the supporters page
  • If sufficient creator interest is demonstrated, we formally establish the union
  • Founding members get first voice in electing representatives
  • The union enters negotiation with Kick on KPP, ad revenue, and creator rights

No dues. No obligations. Expressing interest costs you nothing and commits you to nothing at this stage. It is simply a public statement that you support the idea of creator representation on Kick.
